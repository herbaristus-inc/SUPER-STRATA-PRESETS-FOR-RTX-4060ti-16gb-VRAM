The real monitor, not a slide

The part people skip

This is not a GPU-only trick. The 125B expert set lives in pinned system memory; VRAM is a hot cache. Replace the RAM and the numbers disappear.

Required system: RTX 4060 Ti 16 GB plus 64 GB DDR5 dual channel. With 32 GB the IQ3_S weights spill to the SSD tier and none of these speeds are reachable. The iGPU is the primary display adapter — browsers, Electron and desktop apps are pinned to it via UserGpuPreferences, leaving the whole 4060 Ti for Strata.
